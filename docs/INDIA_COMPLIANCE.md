# India Launch Compliance Workstream — Not Legal Advice

The legal analysis depends on what the project actually does. Publishing open-source node software is different from operating a custodial wallet, exchange, broker, transfer service, payment product or VDA service provider.

Before public launch, obtain advice from Indian counsel on at least:

- PMLA and FIU-IND registration/reporting/AML-CFT obligations if the business model is a covered VDA service provider.
- Customer due diligence, beneficial-owner checks, sanctions screening, record retention, suspicious-transaction reporting and travel-rule requirements where applicable.
- Income-tax treatment and reporting relating to virtual digital assets, including section 115BBH and section 194S where applicable to the transaction and parties.
- Consumer protection, advertising, disclosures and grievance redressal.
- Data protection, cybersecurity, incident reporting and retention obligations.
- FEMA/foreign-exchange and cross-border issues for global payments or offshore counterparties.
- Payment-system regulation if the product starts functioning as a regulated payment system or prepaid instrument; do not assume a blockchain coin is automatically authorized for domestic payments.
- Corporate, accounting, audit, intellectual-property and employment obligations.

Official starting points to verify at launch:

- FIU-IND: https://fiuindia.gov.in/
- Income Tax Department — VDA schedule: https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/schedule_vda
- RBI: https://www.rbi.org.in/

The FIU-IND and tax rules may change. Re-check the current official instruments and obtain a written legal opinion immediately before operating any service. No exchange listing, returns, price or liquidity is promised.
